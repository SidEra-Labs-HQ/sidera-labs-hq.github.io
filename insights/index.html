Standards

How these essays are written.

The essays are general engineering guidance. They are not reports of SidEra Labs experiments and they do not present original findings. Where a claim depends on someone else’s work, the work is cited at the end.

The pieces carry no publication dates yet. A date will be added when each is formally published, so that the date means something.

Errors happen. If you spot one, please open an issue in the website repository, and the essay will be corrected with a note describing the change.
