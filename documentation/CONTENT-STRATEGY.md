# Content Strategy

## Audience
1. **Engineers and researchers** who land on an essay or the GitHub organization and want to know who is behind it and whether it is credible.
2. **Potential collaborators, reviewers and employers** checking what SidEra Labs actually is.
3. **Search and retrieval systems** that need an accurate definition of the organization and clean, citable essays.

## Positioning
> SidEra Labs is an independent engineering and research initiative exploring artificial intelligence, software engineering, agent systems, advanced computing and robotics, and describing what it finds with precision.

Voice: direct, specific, calm, a little dry. Plain technical vocabulary; no hype words; few em dashes; no repeated mission statement. Tagline *Engineering the Next Era of Intelligence.* is used as a direction (footer, social image, Organization `slogan`), never as a claim of achievement.

## Honesty rules applied
- Every research area is labelled **Exploratory** with "no public work yet".
- Essays state they are general guidance, not SidEra experiments, and carry no dates until formally published.
- Projects lists the website repository only, as infrastructure, and says so.
- No testimonials, logos, statistics, awards, partners, addresses, registrations.

## Content structure
Six research areas share one structure: what it investigates, why it matters, important questions, possible applications, status. Essays share another: lede, contents, sections with ids, a short-version note, references, next links.

## Essay plan (written)
1. *The anatomy of an agent loop* (agent systems): workflow vs agent; four jobs; state; tool contracts; budgets; verification; untrusted content; logging.
2. *What a result needs before you believe it* (methodology): question first; baselines; leakage; repeated runs; ablation; slices; records; a ladder of claims; negative results.
3. *Where software meets hardware* (integration): units and frames (Mars Climate Orbiter), timing, safe states, simulation, replay, a test ladder.
Candidates for later (only when there is something real to say): evaluation of a small model on a real task; a write-up of any public SidEra repository.
